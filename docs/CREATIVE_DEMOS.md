# Flagship creative demonstrations

Three fictional Ghanaian businesses, each carried from synthetic evidence and an approved (synthetic)
Brand Kit to a Creative Direction Brief, three structurally different concepts and — since milestone 3C.4
— one complete rendered concept website. Every document and site was generated by the real deterministic
engine; no photograph, page or font file was fetched, and none of these is a real client.

The rendered sites are internal concept prototypes. They carry the “Concept Website — Not an Official
Business Website” disclosure, link only to their own pages and anchors, and turn enquiry, booking and
brief buttons into a prototype notice. No form is submitted, no payment is taken and no analytics runs.
The index is at `/showcase`.

### Why each site looks the way it does

- **Nsroma Atelier — The Cutting Archive (editorial columns).** A warm paper ground, an oxblood accent and
  a high-contrast display serif make the site read as an archive rather than a shop. The promise “Cloth
  remembers the hand” is split across three lines so the italic phrase behaves like a marginal note; the
  lead gives way to a material index, a process chapter and an invitation to begin with a conversation.
  The composition answers the evidence: hand-finished kente and kaba, no published price or lead time,
  and a current site whose testimonial is still a placeholder.
- **Adwoa's House — First Light (cinematic full bleed).** The first frame is a room at dusk, not a logo.
  “Arrive slowly.” sits low against the photograph, and the sequence continues through room, day, the walk
  to Cape Coast Castle and the return. Deep green shade carries the garden; one brass-like accent carries
  the italic word. The restraint is deliberate: nine rooms, a walled garden and a quiet street are the
  recorded facts, so room names and rates stay visibly marked as placeholders.
- **Bantama Design Office — Drawn Off-Axis (asymmetric offset).** A monochrome architectural frame, a
  vertical index rail and oversized grotesque capitals put the plan under pressure. The argument
  “The plan is the argument.” is set as a stack, with the last line in vermilion; drawings and reference
  images are credited as studies, never as built work. The asymmetry is the practice's position —
  drawings before renders, constraint before form — and every project is labelled “Not built”.

## What each document demonstrates

- **A brief that distinguishes evidence from invention.** Verified facts cite observation ids; the
  approved kit cites its version; approved files cite their ids; suggestions and questions are labelled.
- **Three different arguments.** Each business receives three different composition families, motion
  directions, typographic pairings and colour grounds. The tailoring concepts are archival, nocturnal
  and indexical; the hotel's are quiet, cinematic and observational; the architecture practice's are an
  index, an asymmetric argument and a working plan.
- **Typography with a licence and a reason.** Display and body faces come from the OFL catalogue with
  fallback stacks, weights, language coverage and legibility notes. The approved display face is held;
  the supporting voice and scale change.
- **Colour with measured contrast.** Every pair shown passes WCAG AA; the approved accent is respected,
  the ground and support tone belong to the direction.
- **Motion as a specification.** One direction per concept, with duration, easing, properties, a
  reduced-motion alternative and performance limits. No animation code is generated.
- **Conversion and responsive intent.** A named primary action, a proof order, friction to remove, and
  a 360px-first behaviour for each composition.
- **An advisory review.** Twelve dimensions, each with a note, explicitly presented as heuristics rather
  than an objective score.
- **Trade-offs and risks.** Every concept states what it gives up and what could go wrong.
- **A rendered counterpart.** Each business's chosen concept is built by the same blueprint engine and
  trusted renderer as the Studio: five, six and eight pages respectively, working internal navigation,
  self-hosted OFL type, licensed concept media and a visible prototype disclosure.
- **Limitations.** The documents repeat the engine's own limitations: no model, no proprietary font, no
  unapproved imagery, no publication, and human approval still required.

## Regenerating

```bash
npm run demo:creative
```

The script loads `src/lib/domain/creative-demos.ts` with Vite's SSR loader and writes the Markdown and
JSON files here. The output is deterministic; a diff means the engine or the synthetic inputs changed.
